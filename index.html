04 · FAQs

Questions you might have.

Everything you need to know before placing your order.

It's simple! Choose a template you love, click "Preview Design" to see it live, then click "Use This Design". Fill in your event details on the customization form and click Send — your details reach our team instantly on WhatsApp. We then personalize your invitation and deliver a live website link, usually within a few hours.
Most invitations are ready within 2–5 hours of us receiving your details. During peak times (weekends, festive seasons) it may take up to 24 hours. We'll keep you updated via WhatsApp throughout the process.
Standard (₹249) covers all the essentials — couple names, parents, date, time, and venue. Premium (₹499) includes everything in Standard plus a couple story section, photo gallery, Google Maps navigation, an interactive countdown timer, RSVP tracking, and a save-to-calendar button.
Yes! We offer one free revision within 48 hours of delivery. Just message us on WhatsApp with the correction and we'll update it promptly. Additional edits beyond that may carry a small fee depending on the scope.
After submitting your customization form, simply send your couple photos directly to us via WhatsApp. You can share 1–5 high-quality images. We recommend photos that are clear, well-lit, and in portrait orientation for best results on the invitation.
Your invitation is a live website link — no app download needed. You simply share the link on WhatsApp, and guests tap it to open a beautiful full-screen invitation on any phone or computer. It works on all devices and browsers instantly.
Payment is handled directly over WhatsApp via UPI, bank transfer, or any method convenient for you. We only begin work after payment confirmation, so your money is always secure. We'll send you a receipt once payment is received.
